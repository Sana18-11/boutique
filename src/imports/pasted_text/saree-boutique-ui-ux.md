Design a complete **premium saree boutique e-commerce website UI/UX** inspired by the clean, editorial and premium shopping experience of modern Indian fashion e-commerce websites such as Nyra Label.

Do NOT copy the exact branding, logo, images, colors, text, or layout of any existing website. Create an **original design** with a similar level of elegance, clean product presentation, easy navigation and premium fashion-commerce experience.

The website sells **ONLY sarees**.

## BRAND STYLE

Create a sophisticated, luxurious and elegant Indian saree boutique aesthetic.

Use:

* Warm ivory / cream background
* Soft beige
* Champagne / muted gold accents
* Deep wine / maroon as an occasional accent
* Dark charcoal text
* Plenty of white/negative space

Typography:

* Elegant serif font for major headings
* Clean modern sans-serif for body text, navigation and buttons

Visual style:

* Premium
* Minimal
* Feminine
* Sophisticated
* Editorial fashion look
* Clean product photography
* Spacious layouts
* Subtle borders
* Very light shadows
* Refined hover effects

Avoid:

* Neon colors
* Excessive gradients
* Heavy shadows
* Excessive rounded cards
* Cluttered layouts
* Overly decorative Indian patterns
* Cheap-looking e-commerce styling

The final website should feel like a **high-end Indian saree label**, not a generic online store.

# IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES

The website must follow these exact rules:

1. The boutique sells ONLY:

   * Soft Silk Sarees
   * Cotton Sarees
   * Semi Silk Sarees

2. Online payment is mandatory.

3. Payment methods:

   * UPI
   * Credit/Debit Card
   * Net Banking
   * Wallets

4. Cash on Delivery is NOT available.

5. There is NO REFUND policy.

6. Do not design a refund request workflow.

7. Customers can track their orders.

8. Customers can view their own profile and orders.

9. Admin can manage customers, products, inventory and orders.

10. Admin dashboard must be protected and invisible from the normal customer navigation.

11. Customer and admin interfaces are part of the same overall system but have different access permissions.
